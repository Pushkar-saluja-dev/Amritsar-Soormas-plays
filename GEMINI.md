# Development Rules

## Always Check Devtools
After making any visual or structural change to the website, you MUST use the chrome-devtools-mcp integration to evaluate scripts, take screenshots, and visually verify that the layout, styling, and animations are functioning exactly as expected.

## Strict Change Scope
Only make the exact changes explicitly requested by the user. Do not modify, refactor, or touch any other code or files outside of the user's explicit instructions.

## Auto-Commit and Push
Automatically commit and push everything to GitHub after every change you make.
